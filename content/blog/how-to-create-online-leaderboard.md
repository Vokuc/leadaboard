Creating an online leaderboard is one of the most effective ways to boost engagement, whether you're running a remote sales team, managing a local gaming tournament, or tracking fitness challenges.

Gone are the days of manually updating Google Sheets and emailing out screenshots. Today, you can create a live, real-time leaderboard in minutes. Here is how.

## Step 1: Define Your Metric

Before you create your leaderboard, you need to decide what you are actually tracking. A leaderboard is only effective if the scoring is transparent and fair. 

- **In Gaming**: Are you tracking wins, kill/death ratios, or speedrun times?
- **In Business**: Are you tracking revenue closed, calls made, or customer satisfaction scores?
- **In Sports**: Are you tracking total points, goal difference, or match wins?

Keep it simple. The best leaderboards focus on one primary metric that determines the overall ranking, with secondary metrics used as tie-breakers.

## Step 2: Choose Your Platform

While you could build a custom solution from scratch, using a dedicated platform is significantly faster and provides features like real-time WebSocket updates out of the box.

## Step 3: Add Participants and Share

Once your board is designed, it's time to populate it. 

You can manually add your team members, or generate a **Join Link** to let participants register themselves. Once the players are in, simply input their scores via the admin dashboard. 

The beauty of a modern online leaderboard is the **Live Link**. You can take the public URL of your leaderboard and:
- Share it in your company Slack or Discord.
- Cast it directly to a Smart TV in your office.
- Embed it into your WordPress site or Twitch stream using an iframe overlay.

Whenever you update a score in the admin panel, the leaderboard will instantly update for everyone watching, with zero page refreshes required.
