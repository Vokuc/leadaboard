Running an in-house gym competition or fitness challenge is a proven way to increase member retention, build a tight-knit community, and inject some fun into the daily grind. 

Whether it is a CrossFit throwdown, a powerlifting meet, or a 30-day step challenge, the key to success is smooth organization and transparent scoring.

## Step 1: Choose the Format

Your competition needs to be accessible but challenging. Consider your member base before finalizing the workouts.

- **The AMRAP (As Many Reps As Possible)**: Great for pushing members to their absolute aerobic limit within a set time frame.
- **The 1-Rep Max**: Perfect for powerlifting clubs focusing on raw strength.
- **The Month-Long Challenge**: E.g., "Row 50km in November." Excellent for general fitness gyms looking to boost daily attendance.

**Pro-Tip**: Always include scaled divisions! If your competition is too difficult, beginner members will feel alienated. Create an "Rx" (as prescribed) division and a "Scaled" division.

## Step 2: Ditch the Whiteboard

The traditional gym whiteboard is great for the daily WOD, but terrible for a multi-week competition. It gets erased, it's hard to read, and members can't check it from home.

[CTA:Default]

By moving your competition to a digital leaderboard, you gain massive advantages:
1. **Remote Viewing**: Members can check their standings on their phones while recovering on the couch.
2. **Permanent Records**: You can archive the results and reference them for next year's competition.
3. **Instant Filtering**: Instantly filter the board by Men, Women, Scaled, or Rx divisions without doing mental gymnastics.

## Step 3: Run the Event Day

If you are hosting a live throwdown, the energy in the room will be dictated by the leaderboard.

Set up a TV or projector in the gym and cast your digital leaderboard to it. As heats finish their workouts, have your judges input the scores on their iPads or phones. The main screen will update in real-time, letting the crowd know exactly who is in the lead before the final heat even begins. 

This level of professional polish elevates a simple Saturday workout into a memorable, high-stakes event that your members will talk about for months.
