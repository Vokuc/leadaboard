# Creating Your First Competition

Setting up a competition on LeaderboardOS takes less than 60 seconds using our interactive setup wizard. Follow this guide to launch your first event.

---

## Step 1: Open the Creation Wizard

1. Sign in to your account and navigate to `/dashboard`.
2. Click the **"+ New Competition"** button in the header, or visit `/dashboard/create` directly.

---

## Step 2: Choose a Format or Template

You have two options to begin:

### Option A: Use a Pre-configured Template (Recommended)
Click **"Use a Template"** to pre-fill scoring rules and settings tailored for your event:
- **Premier Football League**: Pre-configures 3 points for a win, 1 for a draw, and goal differential tie-breakers.
- **Esports Knockout**: Pre-configures a single-elimination bracket with random seeding.
- **Corporate Sales Sprint**: Pre-configures deal milestones, meeting bonuses, and revenue filters.

### Option B: Build from Scratch
Select one of the three core engine types:
- **Simple Points Leaderboard**
- **League Table**
- **Knockout Tournament**

---

## Step 3: Enter Identity & Custom URL Slug

1. **Title**: Enter a clear name for your event (e.g., *Lagos Winter Esports Cup 2026*).
2. **Description**: Add optional details such as venue location, prize details, or eligibility criteria.
3. **Custom Slug**: Choose your public web address (e.g., `lagos-winter-cup`). This determines your public URL at:
   `https://leaderboardos.com/leaderboards/lagos-winter-cup`
4. **Visibility**:
   - **Public**: Your competition appears in the public Marketplace Directory and is indexable by search engines.
   - **Private**: Accessible only to people who have the direct link or are registered participants.

---

## Step 4: Configure Rules and Finish

1. Review and adjust the default scoring rules (e.g., points awarded for wins or bonus events).
2. Click **"Create Competition"**.
3. You will see a celebratory animation and be redirected immediately to your live management console at `/dashboard/leaderboards/[id]`.
