# Subscription Plans and Usage Limits

LeaderboardOS offers flexible subscription tiers for individual coaches, esports organizers, and professional sports federations.

---

## Tier Comparison Matrix

| Feature | Free Plan | Pro Plan | Business Plan |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Monthly Price** | **₦0 / month** | **₦10,000 / month** | **₦50,000 / month** |
| **Active Competitions** | Up to 2 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Participants per Competition** | Up to 32 | Up to 256 | Unlimited |
| **Competition Engines** | Points, Leagues, Brackets | Points, Leagues, Brackets | Points, Leagues, Brackets |
| **Custom Branding & Colors** | Standard | Custom Logo & Colors | Full White-Label |
| **Developer API Keys** | Read-Only | Read & Write (100 req/min) | High-Throughput (500 req/min) |
| **AI Competition Manager** | Limited Trials | Full Access | Full Access |
| **Webhooks & Deliveries** | ❌ | Up to 5 Webhook Endpoints | Unlimited Webhook Endpoints |
| **Custom Domains** | ❌ | ❌ | Custom Subdomain / CNAME |
| **Staff & Admin Seats** | 1 (Owner) | Up to 3 Admins | Unlimited Staff & Referees |

## Upgrading Your Account

1. Navigate to `/dashboard/billing`.
2. Choose your desired plan (**Pro** or **Business**).
3. Click **"Upgrade with Paystack"**.
4. Complete your secure card or bank payment.
5. Your account limits and entitlements update instantly upon payment confirmation.

---

## Managing Your Subscription

You can view past invoices, download receipts, or cancel your renewal at any time directly from the `/dashboard/billing` portal. Canceling ensures you maintain access through the end of your current billing period.
