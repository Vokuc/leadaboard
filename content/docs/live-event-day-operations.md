# Live Event-Day Operations

On tournament day, organizers and referees need a high-contrast, touch-optimized tool they can operate from a smartphone or tablet on the sidelines.

The **Event-Day Console** provides this exact experience at:
`/dashboard/leaderboards/[id]/event-day`

---

## Event-Day Console Features

- **High-Contrast Touch Controls**: Large, tactile buttons for recording goals, points, fouls, and cards under bright sunlight.
- **Live Match Timers**: One-tap kick-off, pause, extra time, and final whistle controls.
- **Pitch / Court Switcher**: Quickly toggle between different venues (e.g. *Pitch 1*, *Pitch 2*, *Main Arena*).
- **Instant Realtime Sync**: As goals or points are tapped, public spectator screens and embed widgets update with zero delay.

---

## Operating a Match on Event Day

1. Open `/dashboard/leaderboards/[id]/event-day` on your mobile device.
2. Select today's fixture from the schedule queue.
3. Tap **"Kick Off"** when play begins. The match clock starts running.
4. When a team scores:
   - Tap the large `+` button under the scoring team.
   - Select the goal scorer or assister from the roster list.
5. Record cards: Tap **"Yellow Card"** or **"Red Card"** and select the offending player to log fair play points.
6. When the match ends, tap **"Final Whistle"**.
7. Confirm the final score. The match is marked as **Completed**, and league standings update immediately.
