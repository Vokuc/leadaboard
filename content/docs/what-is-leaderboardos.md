# What is LeaderboardOS?

**LeaderboardOS** is an all-in-one competition management platform designed to replace manual spreadsheets, paper brackets, and fragmented score-tracking apps. 

Whether you are running a weekend 5-a-side football league, an esports tournament, a corporate sales sprint, or a continuous crossfit challenge, LeaderboardOS provides the tools to set up rules, generate schedules, track live scores, and display real-time standings in under 60 seconds.

---

## Why LeaderboardOS?

Most competition tools on the internet are siloed:
- Bracket generators do not support round-robin league tables.
- Sports league software does not support gaming XP or points systems.
- Corporate incentive trackers do not offer public spectator views or embedded widgets.

LeaderboardOS unifies these formats under a **single competition core**:
1. **Universal Competition Core**: Shared models for competitors, teams, schedules, score logs, and lifecycle stages.
2. **Pluggable Mechanics**: Switch between Simple Points Leaderboards, Round-Robin Leagues, and Single-Elimination Knockout Brackets effortlessly.
3. **Persistent Competitor Identity**: Every player carries their lifetime stats and verified achievements across tournaments with their personal **Competition Passport**.
4. **Real-Time by Default**: Every score update syncs immediately to public screens, mobile spectators, and embedded iframe widgets without requiring a page refresh.

---

## Who is LeaderboardOS For?

- **Tournament Directors & League Organizers**: Manage multi-week schedules, tie-breakers, team rosters, and prize pools from an intuitive dashboard.
- **Matchday Referees & Field Operators**: Use the touch-friendly mobile **Event-Day Console** to record live scores, goals, and cards directly from the sidelines.
- **Competitors & Players**: Register for tournaments, claim competition profiles, form squads, and share dynamic match result cards on social media.
- **Spectators & Fans**: Follow live rankings from venue screens or mobile devices using instant on-canvas QR codes.

---

## Built-in Demo Sandbox Mode

You can test 100% of LeaderboardOS immediately without entering credit cards or database credentials. The platform includes a zero-config **Demo Sandbox Mode** that stores data locally in your browser. When you are ready for production, connecting a Supabase database enables cloud synchronization and multi-user collaboration.
