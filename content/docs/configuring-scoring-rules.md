# Configuring Scoring Rules

Scoring rules standardize how points are awarded in your competition, ensuring consistency across all organizers and referees.

---

## What is a Scoring Rule?

A scoring rule represents a predefined event in your competition, consisting of:
- **Rule Name**: e.g. "Match MVP", "Deal Closed", "5km Run Completed".
- **Points Delta**: The integer point value added or subtracted (e.g. `+25` or `-10`).
- **Description**: Guidance explaining when this rule applies.
- **Icon / Badge**: Visual tag indicating the category of achievement.

---

## Creating and Editing Rules

1. In your competition dashboard, navigate to the **"Scores"** or **"Rules"** tab.
2. Click **"+ Add Scoring Rule"**.
3. Input the event title, point value, and optional guidelines.
4. Click **"Save Rule"**.

### Supporting Penalties & Deductions
To record disciplinary deductions, foul penalties, or missed quotas, set the point value as a negative number (e.g., `-15 Points for Yellow Card` or `-5 Points for Missed Standup`).

---

## Applying Scoring Presets

When configuring a new board, click **"Apply Preset"** to load recommended rules for your domain:
- **Gaming Preset**: Match Win (`+30`), MVP (`+15`), First Blood (`+5`), Disconnect Penalty (`-20`).
- **Fitness Preset**: Daily Workout (`+10`), Personal Record (`+25`), Hydration Goal (`+5`).
- **Sales Preset**: Demo Scheduled (`+20`), Enterprise Closed (`+100`), Upsell (`+50`).

---

## One-Off Custom Score Entries

If an unexpected situation arises that isn't covered by your predefined rules, organizers can select **"Custom Points"** in the score entry console. When doing so, a mandatory **Reason Note** must be entered to preserve the competition's audit history.
