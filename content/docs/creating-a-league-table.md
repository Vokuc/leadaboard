# Creating a League Table

A **League Table** competition organizes clubs or squads into structured matchdays where every team plays opponents in a round-robin format.

---

## Step 1: Select the League Format

1. Go to `/dashboard/create`.
2. Select the **"League Table"** competition format, or pick the **"Football League"** preset template.
3. Enter your competition title (e.g. *West Coast Basketball League 2026*) and choose your custom URL slug.
4. Click **"Create Competition"**.

---

## Step 2: Register Participating Teams

Before generating a match schedule, add your competing teams:
1. Open your league dashboard and navigate to the **"Teams & Rosters"** tab.
2. Click **"+ Add Team"**.
3. Enter the Team Name (e.g., *Thunderbirds SC*), upload an optional crest/logo, and designate a team captain.
4. Repeat for all competing clubs. We recommend an even number of teams (e.g. 8, 10, or 12). If an odd number of teams register, the scheduling engine automatically assigns one team a **Bye** each round.

---

## Step 3: Configure League Points

In the **"Rules & Scoring"** tab, confirm your league's points allocation:
- **Win**: Default 3 points.
- **Draw (Tie)**: Default 1 point.
- **Loss**: Default 0 points.
- **Bonus / Fair Play Points**: Optional bonus or penalty point adjustments.
