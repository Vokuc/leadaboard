# Team Rosters and Squad Management

Managing team sports, esports squads, and corporate departments requires tools to register players, assign captains, and submit official rosters.

---

## Creating and Managing Teams

From your organizer dashboard or participant passport:
1. Open the **"Teams"** management panel.
2. Click **"+ Create Team"**.
3. Set your Team Name, upload a high-resolution logo or crest, and pick team accent colors.
4. Designate a **Team Captain**: Captains have permissions to invite teammates, set starting lineups, and submit official squad rosters to open leagues.

---

## Adding Players to Rosters

1. Open the team detail view.
2. Add players by entering their email address or searching for their LeaderboardOS **Passport Handle**.
3. Assign jersey numbers, squad roles (e.g., *Forward*, *Goalkeeper*, *Captain*, *In-Game Leader*), and optional player notes.
4. If an invited player does not yet have an account, an invitation link is generated so they can join with a single click.

---

## Team Rebranding & Historical Name Tracking

If a team renames or secures a new title sponsor during the season:
- Updating a team's name preserves all past match results and historical standings.
- LeaderboardOS tracks team name history under the hood so past tournament records remain auditable and accurate.
