# Paid Competition Registrations and Entry Fees

Organizers can monetize their tournaments or leagues by collecting registration entry fees directly from competitors.

---

## Setting Up Paid Registration

1. In your competition dashboard, navigate to the **"Registration"** tab.
2. Toggle **"Enable Public Registration"** to ON.
3. Under **Registration Fee (NGN)**, enter your entry price per player or squad (e.g. `₦5,000`).
4. Set your **Capacity Limit** (e.g., maximum 16 squads).
5. Click **"Save Settings"**.

---

## The Competitor Checkout Experience

When a player visits your public page (`/leaderboards/[slug]`):
1. They click the prominent **"Register Now"** button.
2. They select their squad, enter team details, and accept tournament rules.
3. Clicking **"Proceed to Payment"** triggers the secure **Paystack Checkout Modal**.
4. The competitor pays via Debit Card, Bank Transfer, USSD, or Apple Pay.
5. Upon successful payment verification, their registration is confirmed on the roster, an entry fee transaction is added to your competition ledger, and an automated receipt email is dispatched.

---

## Verifying Registrations

In the dashboard **"Registrations"** console, organizers can inspect all attendees, verify payment reference codes, export attendee lists to CSV, and confirm squad check-in status on matchday.
