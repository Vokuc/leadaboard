# Inviting and Adding Participants

LeaderboardOS gives organizers complete flexibility over how competitors enter a competition: direct manual entry, bulk CSV import, or public self-registration.

---

## 1. Direct Manual Entry

If you are organizing an internal office tournament or a small squad event:
1. Navigate to the **"Participants"** or **"Teams"** tab in your competition console.
2. Click **"+ Add Member"** (or **"+ Add Team"**).
3. Enter the participant's name, optional email address, squad affiliation, and notes.
4. Click **"Save"**. The member immediately appears in your roster and standings table with 0 points.

---

## 2. Bulk CSV Import

For large tournaments or leagues with dozens of players:
1. In the Participants tab, click **"Import CSV"**.
2. Download our sample CSV template, which includes columns for:
   `Name, Email, Team, Notes`
3. Upload your completed `.csv` file.
4. The system validates entries and imports all competitors in a single operation.

---

## 3. Public Self-Registration

To allow players and teams to register themselves online:
1. Open the **"Registration"** tab in your competition settings.
2. Toggle **"Enable Public Registration"** to ON.
3. Configure your registration parameters:
   - **Registration Deadline**: When sign-ups automatically close.
   - **Capacity Cap**: Maximum number of players or teams allowed (e.g. 16 teams).
   - **Entry Fee**: Leave as ₦0 for free entry, or enter a fee to collect registration payments via Paystack.
4. Public visitors can now click **"Register Now"** on your public competition page (`/leaderboards/[slug]`).

---

## Participant Claims

If you add a player manually using their name, that player can later visit the page, click **"Claim This Profile"**, and link their historic match record to their personal **Competition Passport**.
