# Participant Passports and Public Profiles

In traditional tournament software, a player's record is wiped when a tournament ends. LeaderboardOS introduces the **Competition Passport**—a persistent competitor identity that follows players across seasons, leagues, and gaming tournaments.

---

## What is a Competition Passport?

Every competitor receives a public profile accessible via their unique handle:
`https://leaderboardos.com/passport/[handle]` (e.g. `/passport/alex_striker`)

Your passport displays:
- **Lifetime Performance**: Total competitions entered, matches played, win rate percentage, and podium finishes.
- **Skill Ratings (Elo/Glicko)**: Dynamically adjusted ratings calculated from verified head-to-head fixtures.
- **Trophy Cabinet & Badges**: Digital badges awarded for tournament victories, undefeated seasons, or scoring milestones.
- **Squad Memberships**: Active and past teams you have represented.

---

## Claiming a Player Profile

If a league organizer entered your name manually before you created an account:
1. Visit the public leaderboard where you competed.
2. Locate your name in the standings and click **"Claim Profile"**.
3. Sign in or create your LeaderboardOS account.
4. Once verified, all historic matches, goals, and points are instantly linked to your official Competition Passport.
