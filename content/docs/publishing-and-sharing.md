# Publishing and Sharing a Competition

Once your competition is set up, LeaderboardOS offers multiple ways to broadcast live standings, schedules, and brackets to your community.

---

## 1. Sharing the Public URL

Every public competition has a clean, memorable URL:
`https://leaderboardos.com/leaderboards/[your-slug]`

You can copy this link from your dashboard header and share it via WhatsApp groups, Discord servers, email newsletters, or social media.

---

## 2. On-Canvas QR Codes for Live Venues

When hosting an in-person event (such as a sports arena, gym, or gaming cafe):
1. In the competition dashboard or public view, click the **"Share / QR Code"** button.
2. A high-resolution QR code is rendered on screen.
3. Spectators and players can point their phone camera at the screen or printed banner to instantly open live standings on their mobile browsers—with no app install or sign-up required.

---

## 3. Dynamic Social Result Cards

Want to announce the winner of a match or final tournament standings on social media?
1. Click **"Share Result"** on any completed fixture or podium row.
2. LeaderboardOS generates a high-contrast branded graphic showing the final score, team crests, and date.
3. Save the image or click to share directly to Twitter/X, Instagram, or LinkedIn.

---

## 4. Embedding on Your Website

You can embed a live, interactive leaderboard or tournament bracket directly into your club, gym, or company website using an `<iframe>`.

See our full guide on [Embedding Competitions](/docs/embedding-competitions) for instructions and URL customization parameters.
